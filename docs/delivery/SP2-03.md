## Delivered scope

The receiving form captures actual quantity, batch/date, presentation and scoped destination with explicit pending states. Product lookup/manual identification can start a receipt-private line; barcode identification does not count goods or approve catalogue/safety data. Two actual batches for the same code require two explicit lines. Checked pack multiplication is exact and remains a draft estimate.

Same-origin receipt HTTP commands require current Identity authorization, session-bound CSRF and the configured Origin. Kitchen independently checks the individual session and scope. Chef/storekeeper may read/write drafts; cook is denied. Receipt save/reload/list, synthetic scoped destinations, immutable attributable revisions and durable draft-save outcomes are implemented. Aggregate, revision and outcome commit or roll back together. This creates no catalogue approval, stock quantity, movement, reservation, evidence upload or safety disposition.

See the [receiving contract](../architecture/contracts/receiving.md#sp2-03-implementation-boundary) and [permission matrix](../architecture/contracts/individual-access.md#minimal-fixture-permissions). Offline storage remains disabled. The interface explicitly warns that unsaved memory-only fields are cleared at lock/navigation.
